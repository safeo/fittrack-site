Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Introduction

FitTrack ("we", "our", or "the app") is a workout journal that you share with friends. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using FitTrack, you agree to the practices described here.

Information We Collect

  • Account information. Your name and email address, collected when you create an account with email and password, or when you sign in with Apple, Google, or Facebook. If you sign in with a provider, we receive your name and email address from that provider.
  • Workout data. The workouts, exercises, sets, routines, and related notes that you enter in the app.
  • Profile information. Your display name and avatar photo, if you choose to add them.
  • Friend connections. Information about the friends you connect with in the app, so that shared workouts and activity can be shown to you and to them.
  • Health data (optional). If you grant permission, FitTrack can import workout and activity summaries from Apple Health. This import is entirely optional, happens only with your permission, and you can stop it at any time in your device settings.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information only to operate and provide the app's features: keeping your workout journal, syncing it across your devices, showing your profile, and connecting you with your friends in the app.

FitTrack contains no advertising. We do not track you across other apps or websites, we do not sell your personal data, and we do not share your data with third parties for marketing or advertising analytics.

Service Providers

We rely on a small number of providers that process data on our behalf, only as needed to run the app:

  • Supabase — hosting, user authentication, and database storage of your account and workout data.
  • Apple, Google, and Meta (Facebook) — only if you choose to sign in with them, to verify your identity. We receive your name and email address from the provider; we do not receive your password.

Data Retention and Deletion

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. You can permanently delete your account and all of your associated data at any time from within the app (Profile → Delete Account). Account deletion removes your profile, workouts, routines, and friend connections from our systems.

You can also request deletion by emailing us at meerve.tunceer@gmail.com. See our Delete Account page for details.

Security

Your data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored with our hosting provider. Access to your account is protected by your sign-in method.

Children

FitTrack is not intended for children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact us at meerve.tunceer@gmail.com.
