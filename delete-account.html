Delete Your Account

How to permanently delete your FitTrack account and data

Delete in the App (Recommended)

  1. Open FitTrack and go to Profile.
  2. Tap Delete Account.
  3. Confirm the deletion when prompted.

Your account is deleted immediately and permanently. This cannot be undone.

What Is Deleted

  • Your account and sign-in credentials
  • Your profile (display name and avatar)
  • All of your workouts, exercises, and routines
  • Your friend connections in the app
  • Any health data imported into FitTrack from Apple Health (this does not affect the original data in Apple Health itself)

Request Deletion by Email

If you can no longer access the app, you can request account deletion by emailing meerve.tunceer@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account. Please include "Delete my FitTrack account" in the subject line. We will verify your request and delete your account and associated data, and confirm by email when it is done.

Questions

For anything else about your data, see our Privacy Policy or contact us at meerve.tunceer@gmail.com.
